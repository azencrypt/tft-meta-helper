TFT Meta Helper

A free Teamfight Tactics companion app for Overwolf. Status: in development.

Features

Meta comps. Current team compositions with units, items and positioning, aggregated from public high-elo ranked matches. Browse several comps and pick the one you want to play.
In-game overlay. Shows the comp you picked before the match and which of its units you already own. Units of that comp are highlighted when they appear in your own shop.

Data & privacy

  • Meta statistics are computed on our server with the Riot Games API (TFT-LEAGUE-V1, TFT-SUMMONER-V1, ACCOUNT-V1, TFT-MATCH-V1). We publish only aggregated, anonymous data. The API key never leaves the server.
  • In-game state comes from the Overwolf Game Events API.

What it does not do

  • It does not track or show opponents' boards, plays or stats.
  • It does not show lobby or player aggregate stats or augment win rates.
  • It does not give real-time "do this now" instructions or automate input.
  • It has no ads in the game client and is free for everyone.
